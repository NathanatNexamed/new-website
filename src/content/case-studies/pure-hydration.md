## When the report looked better than the lead quality

Pure Hydration came to us with two locations, a reasonable budget, and reports that couldn't be right. Every conversion action in the account was set to primary: page views, button clicks, calls, form fills. Some leads were counted three or four times. On paper the account was converting at over 100%. In practice nobody knew which campaigns were producing patients.

Performance Max was spending thousands a month and producing almost no leads that the front desk could find. Search ads pointed at the homepage, so a patient looking for NAD+ had to find the right page themselves.

## What we changed

We started with tracking, not ads. Calls and form fills became the only primary conversions. Everything else was demoted to secondary so it stopped steering the bidding.

Then we rebuilt the structure:

- **One landing page per service.** IV therapy, injections and NAD+ each got a page that matched the ad and had one clear way to book.
- **Campaigns split by location.** Each location got its own budget and its own reporting, so neither could starve the other.
- **Search only.** We paused Performance Max until there was clean conversion data to learn from.
- **Target CPA with a strict bid cap.** Once the conversion data was trustworthy, we let Google bid, with a ceiling that kept cost per lead in range.

## The result

The documented account results show a 12–15% conversion rate for calls and form fills over a six-month period. That is an advertising conversion metric, not the percentage of clicks that became paying patients. We do not publish a cost-per-patient or appointment lift here because those figures have not been verified for this case study.
