Short answer: one per service you're paying to advertise.

If you run ads for IV therapy, NAD+ and vitamin injections, that's three pages, each matching the ad that sends people there.

## Why the homepage doesn't work

Someone searching "NAD+ therapy near me" has a specific question. If they land on your homepage, they have to find NAD+ in the menu, figure out the price and work out how to book. Every extra step loses people.

A dedicated page answers their question immediately: what it is, how long it takes, what it costs, and one clear way to book.

## What goes on each page

- A headline that repeats what they searched for
- Who it's for and what to expect at the visit
- Price, or a clear starting price
- Provider credentials
- A short FAQ
- One booking action, repeated after the FAQ

## Does it help Google Ads directly?

Yes. Landing page relevance is part of Quality Score, which affects what you pay per click. And conversion tracking gets cleaner when each page maps to one service.
