Every vendor in the medspa space now has an AI feature. Some of them are genuinely useful. Most of the useful ones are invisible to patients.

## Where it helps

- **Summarizing conversations** so the front desk can pick up a lead thread in seconds.
- **Drafting follow-up** that a person reviews and sends.
- **Tagging and routing** inbound messages so the right person sees them first.
- **Reporting**: pulling ad, CRM and EMR data into a monthly summary someone will actually read.

## Where we'd still use a person

The first real conversation with a prospective patient. People choosing a provider for something on their face or in their body want to talk to someone who works there. An AI that pretends to be your front desk tends to be found out, and the trust doesn't come back.

## How we use it

We use automation to make the first response instant and AI to make your team faster. The person who books the consult is still a person.
